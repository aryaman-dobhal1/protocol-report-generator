# TM-104 Wall Thickness Test Method

## 2.1 Equipment
Wall thickness is measured with a calibrated micrometer at the midpoint of each sample.

## 2.2 Conditions
Measurements are taken at 20 ± 2 °C after samples have rested for 30 minutes.

## 3.1 Recording
One measurement is recorded per sample, in millimetres, to 0.01 mm resolution.
